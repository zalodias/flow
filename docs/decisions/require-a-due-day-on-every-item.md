# Require a due day on every item

## Context

Most to-do apps let an item sit without a date in an open-ended backlog. Flow's items can have a due day: a date with no time.

## Decision

Every item has a due day. There are no undated items.

## Why

A date creates commitment. An item without one is a wish that never gets scheduled. Required dates also mean every item has a day it belongs to.
